- Different Rolled Homogeneous Armour (RHA) thicknesses for small caliber weapons used in the game Arma3 designed for tests with [arma3diag_x64.exe](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_3:_Diagnostics_Exe) (Arma3 Dev branch) and `"Shots" diag_enable true;` or `"Shots" diag_enable false;` (Editor missions for each thickness included).

- Formula for `caliber` (penetration, perforation capacity) value: [Bohemia Interactive Community Wiki](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Config_Properties_Megalist#caliber)

- Examples from manufacturers datas:
  - 5.56mmx45mm Armor Piercing 3 (M995), 3.4g (52gr), MV 1030m/s: [12mm RHA at 100m](https://www.nammo.com/product/our-products/ammunition/small-caliber-ammunition/5-56mm-series/5-56-mm-x-45-armor-piercing-3/)
  - 5.56mmx45mm Armor Piercing 45, 4.5g (69gr), MV 900m/s: [7mm RHA at 200m](https://www.nammo.com/product/our-products/ammunition/small-caliber-ammunition/5-56mm-series/5-56-mm-x-45-armor-piercing-45/)
  - 7.62mmx51mm Armor Piercing 8 (M993), 8.3g (128gr), MV 930m/s: [18mm RHA at 100m, 7mm RHA at 500m](https://www.nammo.com/product/our-products/ammunition/small-caliber-ammunition/7-62mm-series/7-62-mm-x-51-armor-piercing-8-m993/)
